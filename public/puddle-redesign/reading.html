Life in London 🇬🇧

A2 · Reading passage

London is the capital city of the United Kingdom. It is a large and exciting city with many famous places. People can visit Big Ben, Tower Bridge, and Buckingham Palace.

There are many parks in London. People often walk, ride bikes, or have picnics when the weather is nice. The city also has buses, trains, and the Underground, so it is easy to travel.

London is home to people from many countries. You can hear different languages and try food from around the world. It is a wonderful place to learn about history and modern life.
